# Financial flow and production gaps

Reviewed 28 November 2026 against main at 7e69e48a. Scope covers the Flutter entry points, authenticated and internal API routes, service bindings, Prisma models and migrations, scheduled recovery, staff controls, repository tests and Android release evidence. Runtime secrets, production database grants and rows, provider contracts, actual tester purchases and bank transfers were outside the evidence available for this review. The PostgreSQL and widget suites below are test evidence to use in acceptance testing. They were not rerun for this documentation change.

## Follow one purchase through the system

| Step | What happens on main | What proves the next boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recharge | Wallet and gift sheet open the Play coin panel. The API supplies product IDs and coin counts. Play supplies the storefront offer and price. The panel listens for purchase updates and submits the token for server verification. | On a Play-installed build, test an available pack, missing offer, cancellation, pending purchase, restart and account switch. Record a sanitized order reference and credit result. |
| Verify and credit | The purchases service checks the flag, catalogue and Google response, including purchased state, product and account binding. It records the purchase, source evidence, coin grant and balanced KCOIN transaction. Consumption follows the durable credit. Replaying the token must credit once. | Interrupt after credit and before consumption, retry, then reconcile the same order, credit and consumed state. |
| Wallet and rewards | Spendable coins combine legacy complimentary, approved reward and purchased balances. Purchased balance includes net recovery adjustments. Rewards originate in the nomination/approval flow. No weekly refill. The display estimate uses the quoted 100-coin pack in the matching currency. | Exercise each source independently and together. A wallet display preference does not set checkout currency or a bank exchange rate. |
| Gift and earn | Post and comment gifts lock the sender wallet, check the request ID, snapshot the gift and write spend and creator entries together. Spend order is legacy, reward, purchased. New purchased spends allocate oldest eligible credits first. Purchased and approved reward gifts create qualifying creator earnings. Legacy proceeds remain separate. | Reconcile mixed-source and concurrent gifts to sender debits, recipient earnings and retained source records after social deletion. Establish how approved reward liabilities are funded. |
| Refund, chargeback and correction | Authenticated RTDN and a scheduled voided-purchases sweep feed verification. The independent refund worker retries queued verification. Staff can review chargeback evidence, preview an order, post eligible recovery and correct a debit. The current posting path uses proven unspent coins on the original order. Shortfalls are recorded. | Exercise missed notification, replay, partial refund, already-gifted coins, competing orders and correction. Keep wallet posting separate from automated evidence recovery. Test buyer notices and staff support lookup against the same reference. |
| Earnings to bank | Earnings holds and reservation lines exist. The withdrawal service validates a minimum, active owned destination and expiring quote, reserves earnings and records a withdrawal. Staff routes handle approval request, separate approval and submission readiness. Submission and webhook reconciliation services model transfer attempts and terminal success/failure. | Implement the creator and provider entry points. Run a sandbox transfer, then prove that retries, unknown outcomes, confirmed failure and a later bank return each leave the correct available balance. |
| Fiat close | No complete trace from Play settlement to bank cash, creator payable and payout settlement was found. A KCOIN reversal or paid payout status alone cannot close the corresponding fiat books. | Match actual report lines, provider settlements and bank totals by currency and period, including fees, tax treatment, FX, adjustments and exceptions. |

## Material gaps at the seams

1. **The wallet currently shows gross qualifying earnings.** GiftsService.balances sums PURCHASE_EARN and REWARD_EARN for withdrawableEarned. It does not subtract active holds, reservations or prior settlements. A withdrawal button currently opens an availability explanation. Before enabling it, serve available, held, reserved and settled amounts from the earnings allocation logic and cap requests server-side. Test a second withdrawal after the first settles.
2. **Transaction history is a partial projection.** GET wallet/transactions reads the latest 50 CoinEntry rows without a cursor. Recovery notices come from a separate source. Build a stable paginated activity view spanning grants, spends, earnings, recoveries, corrections and withdrawals, with references and explicit units. Reconcile opening balance plus movements to closing balance per bucket.
3. **The ledger foundation is real, but coverage differs by event.** Database migrations enforce balanced transactions at commit and immutable journal, audit and source records. The purchase and refund services call the ledger inside their transaction. Gift and reward paths use coin/source records and creator allocations. The legacy reconciliation service exposes a dry run. Define event ownership and carry out an idempotent bridge or posting cutover with totals per source and explicit exceptions for missing history. Avoid double-posting existing purchase grants.
4. **Payout orchestration stops before the bank.** The module binds disabled FX, bank and webhook adapters. A verified bank-destination onboarding flow, creator request/status API, production submission caller and authenticated provider ingress must be connected. Reconciliation recognizes pending, succeeded and failed events. Add a distinct post-settlement return path, plus provider status lookup for ambiguous submissions. A timeout cannot release funds for another payout without resolving the first transfer.
5. **Kyron commission and Play fees need distinct financial events.** The gift path creates creator units equal to the funded spend. Define exactly where Kyron’s 15% applies, its base, rounding, funding for rewards, and how fees are disclosed in the quote. Import Play’s actual fee and settlement amounts. Record creator liability, platform revenue and settlement variances separately. Require approved posting examples in minor units for each supported currency.
6. **Chargeback referral has narrower powers than the approved policy.** Staff review, shortfall history, original-order recovery and compensating corrections exist. A time-limited gifting/purchase restriction, expiry, appeal, and enforcement at entry points still need implementation if used for launch. Preserve delivery of an already-paid legitimate purchase during restriction handling. Keep cross-order offsets and negative balances disabled unless their separate evidence, disclosure and authorization requirements are met.
7. **Market and operational activation remain open.** Approve the first buyer and creator markets, age eligibility, onboarding, payout minimum, FX source, provider and support ownership. Apply the decision in authenticated backend operations. Validate migration state, runtime-role privileges, backup restoration, reconciliation schedules, alarms, key ownership and shutdown controls in the deployed environment. Continue processing in-flight purchases, settlements and returns during a pause.

## Delivery order and release evidence

| Order | Deliverable | Acceptance gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Correct the earnings availability API and wallet labels. In parallel, run signed Play tests and select a bank provider and first payout market. | Held, reserved, settled and legacy earnings cannot be requested. The 0.1.51 Android release, GitHub publish and actual Play testing upload step succeeded in [release run 45887762726](https://github.com/KyronLabs/kyron/actions/runs/45887762726). No completed tester purchase, production rollout or live bank settlement was verified by this audit.
